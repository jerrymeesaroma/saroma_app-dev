**Favorite Line**

*I used to say 'I live my life a quarter mile at a time'. And I think that's why we were brothers... because you did too. No matter where you are... whether it's a quarter-mile away... or halfway across the world... you'll always be with me... and you'll always be my brother.*

**Why I Love this Movie**

*Following Paul Walker’s tragic passing during filming, the movie gave his character, Brian O'Conner, a heartfelt and respectful retirement rather than a dark ending. The split-road montage and "See You Again" song offer one of the most tear-jerking goodbyes in modern blockbuster history.*
